1. NEW NORMALIZED DATABASE TABLE FOR MILESTONES
------------------------------------------------
Instead of storing milestones in a JSON/JSONB text column, the system will use a normalized database table `contract_milestones` to track project progress, deadlines, and payments cleanly.

Table Schema: `contract_milestones`
- `milestone_id` SERIAL PRIMARY KEY
- `contract_id` INTEGER NOT NULL REFERENCES contracts(contract_id) ON DELETE CASCADE
- `title` VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL
- `amount` NUMERIC(15, 2) NOT NULL (Price/amount for this milestone)
- `start_date` DATE (Planned start date)
- `end_date` DATE (Planned deadline/due date)
- `status` VARCHAR(50) DEFAULT 'Pending' (Work status: 'Pending' or 'Completed')
- `payment_status` VARCHAR(50) DEFAULT 'Pending' (Payment/escrow release: 'Pending' or 'Paid')
- `created_at` TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
- `updated_at` TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP

2. THE THREE GIG BUYING FLOWS
------------------------------
Clients can purchase a freelancer's gig in three distinct ways depending on terms and features enabled:

FLOW 1: Direct Buy (Normal Buy - No Milestones)
- The client purchases the gig at the listed price directly without milestones.
- **Payment Rule**: The client pays **100% of the total price upfront** upon order creation.
- **Escrow**: The 100% payment is held in the admin/system escrow wallet.
- **Contract & Milestones**: A contract is created in `contracts`. A single default milestone of 100% price is inserted into the `contract_milestones` table.
- **Release**: When the contract is completed, the client releases the payment. The admin escrow wallet releases 100% of the budget minus the platform commission (e.g., 10% fee) to the freelancer's wallet.

FLOW 2: Price/Requirements Negotiation
- If the freelancer enabled the `negotiation` toggle when creating the gig:
  - The client can click "Negotiate" and request a custom budget, deadline, or terms.
  - A negotiation proposal is sent to the freelancer.
  - Once both parties agree and the freelancer clicks Accept, it triggers either Flow 1 (if no milestones) or Flow 3 (if custom milestones are defined).

FLOW 3: Custom Milestones & Extra Requirements
- The client orders the gig specifying custom requirements and a checklist of custom milestones.
- For each custom milestone, they define:
  * Title/description of work.
  * Price/Amount (sum of all milestones must equal the total gig budget).
  * Start date & end date (deadlines).
- **Payment Rule**: The client pays **50% of the total budget upfront** to initiate the contract.
- **Escrow**: The 50% upfront amount is held in the admin/system escrow wallet.
- **Contract & Milestones**: A contract is created in `contracts` at 50% progress. The custom milestones list is inserted into the `contract_milestones` table.
- **Release**: When the freelancer completes the tasks:
  1. The client is charged the remaining **50% balance amount** of the budget.
  2. The admin escrow wallet releases the first 50%.
  3. Platform commission (e.g., 10% fee) is calculated on the total budget and retained in the system wallet.
  4. The freelancer receives the net earnings (`total budget - commission`) directly into their wallet.

---

3. PAYMENTS & CHECKOUT (STRIPE / PAYPAL OR WALLET)
---------------------------------------------------
- If a client has sufficient balance in their local platform wallet, they can choose to pay via "Wallet Balance".
- If their wallet balance is insufficient, or they choose to pay directly with a credit card:
  - The checkout offers **Stripe** or **PayPal** payment gateways.
  - Choosing Stripe/PayPal executes a simulated instant card charge.
  - The system automatically credits the client's wallet with the required transaction amount (e.g., the 50% upfront deposit or 100% full payment) to cover the charge.
  - The transaction then runs smoothly without throwing "Insufficient wallet balance" errors, recording proper credit card deposit and escrow transfer logs in `wallet_transactions`.
