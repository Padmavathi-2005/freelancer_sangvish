================================================================================
BUY2LANCER PLATFORM NOTIFICATION, SITE MESSAGE & EMAIL TEMPLATES
================================================================================

This document stores the copy texts and template structures for notifications,
messages, and emails sent when actions occur across projects, gigs, and direct hires.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HIRED FREELANCER TEMPLATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[A] IN-APP NOTIFICATION
Title: Proposal Accepted! 🎉
Message: Congratulations! Your proposal on the project "{project_title}" has been accepted by the client. A contract has been started.

[B] PLATFORM CHAT MESSAGE (Automated from Site/Platform)
Message: 
[Site Name] Notification Center:
You have been successfully hired by {client_name} for the project "{project_title}"!
- Bid Amount: {bid_amount}
- Delivery Timeline: {delivery_days} days
A workspace and milestone contract have been created. Please coordinate with the client regarding the deliverables in this chat.
Site Logo: {site_logo}

[C] EMAIL TEMPLATE
Subject: You've been hired on {site_name}! - {project_title}
Body:
Dear {freelancer_name},

Great news! The client {client_name} has accepted your proposal and hired you for their project "{project_title}" on {site_name}.

Project Details:
- Project Name: {project_title}
- Budget: {bid_amount}
- Delivery Days: {delivery_days}

Next Steps:
Go to your Dashboard on {site_name} to view the contract details and track milestones.

Best regards,
The {site_name} Team
Contact: {email_id}
Logo: {site_logo}

3. PROJECT MILESTONE & CONTRACT STAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[A] MILESTONE WORK SUBMITTED
- IN-APP NOTIFICATION
  Title: Work Submitted for Milestone 🚀
  Message: Freelancer {freelancer_name} submitted work for milestone "{milestone_title}" (contract: "{contract_title}"). Please review and approve.
- PLATFORM CHAT MESSAGE
  Message: [{site_name} Platform Message] Work has been submitted and payment approval requested by the freelancer for milestone: "{milestone_title}" under contract: "{contract_title}".
  - Freelancer: {freelancer_name}
  - Client: Please review completed work and click "Approve & Release" inside the project milestones tracker.
- EMAIL TEMPLATE
  Subject: Milestone Work Submitted - {contract_title}
  Body:
  Dear {client_name},

  Freelancer {freelancer_name} has submitted the completed deliverables for milestone "{milestone_title}" on the contract "{contract_title}".

  Please log in to {site_name} to review the submitted work. If it meets your requirements, you can approve the milestone and release the funded escrow payment of {amount}.

  Best regards,
  The {site_name} Team

[B] MILESTONE APPROVED & ESCROW RELEASED
- IN-APP NOTIFICATION
  Title: Milestone Payment Released! 💸
  Message: Client approved and released {amount} for milestone: "{milestone_title}".
- PLATFORM CHAT MESSAGE
  Message: [{site_name} Platform Message] Payment of {amount} has been successfully approved and released for milestone: "{milestone_title}".
  - Sender: {client_name}
  - Recipient: {freelancer_name}
  - Contract: {contract_title}
- EMAIL TEMPLATE
  Subject: Payment Released for Milestone - {contract_title}
  Body:
  Dear {freelancer_name},

  Great news! The client {client_name} has approved the deliverables and released the payment of {amount} for the milestone "{milestone_title}" on the contract "{contract_title}".

  The net amount has been credited to your platform wallet balance and is available for withdrawal.

  Best regards,
  The {site_name} Team

[C] CONTRACT CANCELLED & ESCROW REFUNDED
- IN-APP NOTIFICATION
  Title: Contract Cancelled
  Message: Client cancelled the contract "{contract_title}" and requested a full refund.
- PLATFORM CHAT MESSAGE
  Message: [{site_name} Platform Message] This contract has been cancelled and the escrow amount of {amount} has been fully refunded back to the client's wallet.
  - Reason: Escrow refund due to contract cancellation
- EMAIL TEMPLATE
  Subject: Contract Cancelled & Refunded - {contract_title}
  Body:
  Dear {freelancer_name},

  We are writing to notify you that the client {client_name} has cancelled the contract "{contract_title}" and requested a full refund of the escrow balance.

  The contract is now closed, and all active milestones have been marked as cancelled.

  Best regards,
  The {site_name} Team

[D] DISPUTE CONTESTED
- IN-APP NOTIFICATION
  Title: Dispute Contested ⚖️
  Message: Freelancer contested the refund request for contract "{contract_title}". The dispute is now under review.
- PLATFORM CHAT MESSAGE
  Message: [{site_name} Platform Message - Dispute Contested] Freelancer {freelancer_name} has contested the refund request and provided an explanation:
  "{explanation}"
  An admin mediator will review the case.
- EMAIL TEMPLATE
  Subject: Dispute Contested - Action Required - {contract_title}
  Body:
  Dear {client_name},

  The freelancer {freelancer_name} has contested your refund request for the contract "{contract_title}".

  The case has been escalated to our dispute resolution team. An admin mediator has been added to your contract chat room to help reach a fair verdict.

  Best regards,
  The {site_name} Team

[E] DISPUTE RESOLVED - BUYER WINS
- IN-APP NOTIFICATION
  Title: Dispute Resolved: Buyer Wins ⚖️
  Message: Dispute resolved for contract "{contract_title}". Escrow refunded to client.
- PLATFORM CHAT MESSAGE
  Message: [{site_name} Platform Message - Dispute Resolved] Verdict: Buyer Wins. Dispute has been resolved. Entire escrow amount has been returned to the client.
- EMAIL TEMPLATE
  Subject: Dispute Resolved - {contract_title}
  Body:
  Dear {freelancer_name},

  The dispute on contract "{contract_title}" has been resolved. The admin mediator has ruled in favor of the client, and the escrow amount has been refunded back to their wallet.

  Best regards,
  The {site_name} Team
